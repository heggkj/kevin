Method & theme definitions
Provisional, overlapping keyword themes, matched against target_long, not sender descriptions. Each entry counts once per matching theme. Bars show DART and PAT shares within a theme; the percentage is DART ÷ (DART + PAT) × 100. Three entries labeled both are excluded. Unmatched entries are retained in the collection baseline. Mentions can be incidental; these are exploratory associations, not manually validated topic labels or causal effects. Pooled results do not adjust for uneven coverage over time.
